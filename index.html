About

I’ve always been drawn to games, from streaming the ones I love to bringing their characters to life through cosplay. Eventually, that curiosity shifted from wanting to experience games to wanting to understand how they’re made, and especially how the characters inside them come alive.

That led me to Howest DAE, where I’m specializing in Game Development and turning that passion into a career. I’m particularly interested in gameplay AI programming, while also exploring engine development and the systems that make games work behind the scenes.

Outside of game development, I enjoy watching anime and traveling. I’ve had the opportunity to visit countries including Ireland, Italy, Malta, and more.

I speak Bulgarian (native) and English (fluent, C1).
